\keys{COVID-19, death prediction, monography}

\begin{abstract}{In this study, a systematic mapping was accomplished in order to find predictive models of deaths as vaccination was introduced in the population. In the analyzed articles, modified SIR(Susceptible - Infected - Removed) models and variations, as well as NN(Neural Networks) models and modifications. In addition, was observed through data from OpenDataSus website the advancement of vaccination in the Midwest region and a prediction model based on the LSTM algorithm with data from the State of Goiás was proposed.}
\end{abstract}
